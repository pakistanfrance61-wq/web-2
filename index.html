Community Standards

Rehan Server Rules

Please review these before applying. These rules protect everyone in the community.

1. Be Respectful & Friendly

Treat all members with kindness. Harassment, racism, slurs, toxicity, personal attacks, and hate speech result in an immediate ban. Disagree with respect.

2. No Spam, Unsolicited DMs, or Self-Promotion

Do not send unsolicited server invites or advertise in members' private DMs. Use designated #self-promo or creator channels for sharing content.

3. Safe & Age-Appropriate (13+ Discord ToS)

All members must be 13 or older per Discord's Terms of Service. Absolutely no NSFW, gore, doxxing, cheating tools, or illegal content.

4. Keep Channels Organized & Respect Voice Lounges

Post content in its appropriate channel. Avoid mic spam, high-pitched screamers, or soundboard flooding in public voice rooms.

5. Listen to Staff & Moderator Decisions

Staff members ensure the community remains safe. If you have an issue with a moderator's decision, open a support ticket or DM an admin privately.

    You can join immediately using the "Join Server" button above! The application form gives you prioritized member roles, access to exclusive channels, and whitelist status for giveaways and tournaments.
